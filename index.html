English

The Game Master app (com.oliviervilledieu.gamemaster), developed by Olivier Villedieu, does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

  • No Internet connection: the app does not request Internet access and does not communicate with any server.

  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party services.
  • No sensitive permissions (location, contacts, camera, microphone…) are requested.
